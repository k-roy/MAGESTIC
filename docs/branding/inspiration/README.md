# Branding inspiration

Reference imagery to draw on for future MAGESTIC schematics and branding art.
These are mood / style references, **not** package assets — the shipped mark
lives in `../magestic_mark.svg`.

## `barcode_dna_helix_donor_recruitment.png`

A double helix whose base-pair rungs morph into a **scannable barcode**, with a
second strand (donor, in red) being recruited below — the two ideas at the heart
of MAGESTIC fused into one image: **the integrated, trackable barcode** and
**donor recruitment**. Deep-ocean blue/teal palette with a single warm (yellow)
accent at the recruitment beads.

Why it's worth keeping: it captures the "barcode *is* the DNA" concept far more
literally than an abstract striped glyph, and the blue→teal + warm-accent palette
is close to the locked Indigo-Cyan brand. A future banner or hero illustration
could echo the helix-to-barcode transition.
